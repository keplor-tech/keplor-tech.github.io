# Brand Guidelines

## Personality
Thoughtful, precise, forward-looking, calm, capable. Prefer clarity over hype.

## Color
- Ink `#050507`: primary canvas.
- Keplor Purple `#36006D`: core identity.
- Near-black surfaces `#0A090F`.
- Main text `#F4F0FA`; secondary `#AAA3B8`.
- Use violet/indigo/blue/cyan as localized light, not broad saturated fills.

## Composition
- Large editorial headlines.
- Generous negative space.
- Fine dividers and small uppercase metadata labels.
- Open service rows instead of defaulting to card grids.
- One signature visual per major composition.

## Prohibited drift
Cream/orange rebrands, excessive gradients, glass panels everywhere, generic bento grids, fake metrics, random 3D spheres, gratuitous stars/grids, overdecorated backgrounds, and unverified marketing claims.
