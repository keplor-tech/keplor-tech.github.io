# Final Source Register

| Source | Role in the handoff | Verified public URL |
|---|---|---|
| Founder-provided Canva cover / gradient / objective / contact references | Primary visual authority | Packaged locally under `references/` |
| Google Stitch | Design-system and iterative prototyping process; not a visual theme to copy | https://stitch.withgoogle.com/ |
| Google Labs — “Introducing ‘vibe design’ with Stitch” (18 March 2026) | Infinite canvas, design intent/context, `DESIGN.md`, connected prototypes and iterative refinement | https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/stitch-ai-ui-design/ |
| Google Labs — “We’re introducing new ways to design in real time with Stitch” (19 May 2026) | Real-time iteration and design-to-development handoff | https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/stitch-updates/ |
| Lium homepage | Direct value proposition, named capability progression, domains, featured study, FAQs and consistent CTA | https://www.lium.ai/ |
| Lium platform | Layered explanation of how a product is organized | https://www.lium.ai/platform |
| Lium about | Company description and public company information | https://www.lium.ai/about |

## Method and limitations

- The Stitch findings are based on Google's official public product page and its official Google Labs articles.
- The Lium findings are based on publicly accessible text and linked pages from Lium's live website as reviewed on 24 October 2026. The observations primarily describe information hierarchy and messaging; they do not assert private implementation details.
- The guide intentionally avoids reproducing third-party site copy or assets. Only high-level principles are carried into the Keplor-specific design.
